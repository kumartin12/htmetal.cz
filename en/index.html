An experienced team, modern technology, utmost precision.

HT Metal s.r.o. specialises in metalworking – milling, turning and grinding on conventional and CNC machines. We also offer further services around metal processing.

The company was founded in 2010 and builds its production on the experience and expertise of people who have worked in metalworking for many years. With a well-equipped machine park and qualified staff, we manufacture professionally machined parts with utmost precision.

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers before you write to us.

How do I send an enquiry?

Email htmetal@seznam.cz or call one of the managing directors. A drawing or description, the material and the quantity help us respond quickly.

Where are you based and who do you supply?

We are based in Horšovský Týn in the Plzeň Region. We supply Czech and foreign companies, for example ŠKODA Power and STOPA Anlagebau.

Which certifications do you hold?

We are ISO 9001 certified. You can download the certificate in the Certifications section. AS/EN 9100 for aerospace is in preparation.

Which machines do you use?

A Mori Seiki NLX 2500ST CNC turning centre and a KAS MCV 1270 Power vertical machining centre. We program in HSMWorks.

Do you also offer surface treatment and heat treatment?

Yes, both are part of our metalworking services. Send us your requirements and we will agree on the procedure.
